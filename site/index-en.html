From scribbled note to a recipe that works.

On the back of a ticket. In a notebook that sticks together after ten years of service. In a photo you once took at the pass. Those notes are your kitchen’s knowledge, and they get lost, fade, or can only be read by the person who wrote them.

Savour is made to keep that knowledge. Not by retyping everything, but by turning your notes, binders and files into recipes that are right and that your whole brigade can read, in one go.

Questions about Savour

Which app do I use to organise recipes for my restaurant?

Savour is made for professional kitchens: you import your existing recipes, organise them in folders and share them with your team. You start for free.

How do I digitise handwritten recipes?

Take a photo of the recipe and upload it to Savour. The AI turns it into a clean recipe with ingredients and method, which you then check.

Can I import recipes from Word or PDF?

Yes. Even a file with hundreds of recipes at once; Savour splits them up itself.

How do I share recipes with my kitchen team?

With a kitchen team. You invite colleagues and give them rights to view or to edit.

Is Savour suitable for recipe management in hospitality?

Yes. Savour is built for restaurants, hotels and catering: manage recipes centrally, share them with your kitchen team and organise them by section, menu or location.

Does Savour work offline?

Yes. Open Savour once with internet and add it to your home screen; after that you can open your recipes without a connection, for example in the walk-in. Editing works again as soon as you are online.

Where are my recipes stored?

In the cloud, kept separate per account. You open them on computer, tablet and phone, and only you and the people you invite to a kitchen team can read them.

Can I print a whole recipe folder at once?

Yes. Print single recipes or a complete recipe folder at once, neatly laid out for the binder in the kitchen. With Savour Pro your restaurant’s logo is on it.

Is Savour for home cooks too?

It can be, but Savour is built for the professional kitchen: big recipe books, teams and service.

Can you import my recipes for me?

Yes. With the import service, the chefs behind Savour personally move your whole recipe folder over: imported, organised in your folders and every recipe checked. From €85 one-off, together with a yearly Team 5, 10 or 25 plan. The first 5 kitchens are imported for free.

How does the import service work?

You request it on the website. Once approved you get a personal link: you pay the team plan and the package, then send us your recipes, your folder structure and the email addresses of your colleagues. We import and check everything. As soon as your team is ready, everyone gets an email to choose a password.

INGREDIËNTENLIJST

Nieuwe ingrediënten

Namen uit recepten die Savour nog niet kent. Je ziet alleen de naam, in hoeveel accounts hij voorkomt en de taal; nooit het recept, de hoeveelheid of wie het is. Pas wat je goedkeurt, komt in de ingrediëntenlijst en wordt bij iedereen herkend, met de allergenen die jij vastlegt. Wat je afwijst (geen ingrediënt, een basisrecept, een tikfout), komt niet meer terug. Bij elke naam leest Savour mee welk ingrediënt erachter zit (“aardbeien coulis” → Aardbei); met Koppelen neem je dat in één klik over.

IMPORT SERVICE

Aanvragen en aanleveringen

1. Aanvraag binnen (ook per mail): keur goed met een prijs (0 = gratis) of wijs af. 2. De klant krijgt een mail, betaalt het teamabonnement en het pakket, en levert daarna recepten, mappen en de e-mailadressen van de leden aan. 3. Lees de bestanden hier in met de Savour-lezer, kijk ze na en laad ze in het keukenteam. 4. Geef het keukenteam vrij: iedereen krijgt een mail om een wachtwoord te kiezen, en de aangeleverde bestanden en gegevens worden gewist.
