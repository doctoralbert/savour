Printing recipes

Print one recipe for the wall, or a complete recipe folder for the binder. Quick, neat and always the version that is right.

  • One recipe or a whole folderAt once, in the order of your folder.
  • Readable from a distanceA calm layout, even with a stain on it.
  • With your own logoWith Savour Pro at the top of every page.

Print a single recipe

Open a recipe and choose Print. You get one calm page with title, yield, ingredients and method. Scaled the recipe, for example to 40 covers? Then you print exactly those quantities.

Print a complete recipe folder

All the ice cream recipes need to go in the pastry binder? Open the folder and print it in one go: every recipe on its own page, in the order of your folder. No more fifty files one by one.

Calm layout

Large and clear. Readable from a metre away, even with a stain on it.

Always the latest version

Because you print from Savour, the version on the wall is always the one that is right now. The print date is at the bottom, so you can see whether a binder is still up to date.

With your restaurant’s logo

With Savour Pro every page carries your restaurant’s logo and name at the top. Within a team the logo applies to everyone. Choose A4 for the binder or A5 for a small folder at the pass.

When paper works better

A phone is handy, but not welcome everywhere: nobody wants to touch one while working on meat or fish, and some kitchens use fixed binders per section. So print what belongs on the wall or in the binder, and keep Savour as the source of the latest version.

Keeping binders current

Agree when a binder is renewed, for example at every menu change. Remove the old print as soon as the new one is there; two versions side by side cause mistakes. The print date at the bottom of the page shows at a glance which one is newest.

What a print looks like

At the top your restaurant’s logo and name, below that ingredients and method side by side. At the bottom the print date, so you can see whether the binder is still current.

A whole folder at once

Open a folder and print it in one go, with a table of contents and the number of each recipe at the bottom of the page.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose between A4 and A5?

Yes. A4 for the binder in the kitchen, A5 for a small folder at the pass.

How do I put my logo on the print?

With Savour Pro under Settings → Logo on prints. In a team the owner’s logo applies to everyone.

Do I print the converted quantities?

Yes. If you converted a recipe, you print exactly those quantities.
