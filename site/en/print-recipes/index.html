Home / Features / Printing and binders

Print recipes, even a whole folder at once

Sometimes paper on the wall simply works best. Then it has to be quick and neat.

A whole folder at once

All the ice cream recipes need to go in the pastry binder? Print the whole folder at once, instead of fifty files one by one.

Calm layout

Title, yield, ingredients and method, large and clear. Readable from a metre away, even with a stain on it.

Always the latest version

Because you print from Savour, the version on the wall is always the one that is right now. The print date is at the bottom, so you can see whether a binder is still up to date.

With your restaurant’s logo

With Savour Pro every page carries your restaurant’s logo and name at the top. In a team the logo applies to everyone. Choose A4 for the binder or A5 for a small folder at the pass.
