A recipe in parts

A composed dish in one recipe, with each component’s own ingredients and method.

On this pageWhen to use partsAdding a partWhen importingScaling and printingOr rather a base recipe

When to use parts

For a dish made of components: a tart with dough, filling and glaze, or a plate with meat, sauce and garnish. Everything is in one recipe, and still everyone knows what belongs to which component.

Adding a part

Choose “＋ Add part” in the recipe. Give the part a name, such as “Dough”, and fill in its own ingredients and method. Add as many parts as you like.

When importing

A line ending in a colon, such as “Dough:”, is recognised as the heading of a part when importing. Headings from Mela, Flavorish and CookBookManager become parts too.

Scaling and printing

All parts come along when scaling. On the print, each part appears with its name above its own ingredients and method.

Or rather a base recipe

Using a component in many recipes, like a stock or a base dough? Make it a recipe of its own and link it. Then you change it in one place.
