Importing photos and handwritten notes

The note at the pass, your culinary school notebook or a page from a cookbook: one photo and it’s in.

On this pageUploading photosUsing the cameraWhat the AI doesThe original is keptSeveral photos in a zipTips for a good photoiPhone photos (HEIC)

Uploading photos

Choose “Add recipe” → “Import recipe” → “Photos” and pick one or more photos (.jpg, .png or .webp). Each photo is read as a recipe. This works with Savour Pro, up to 500 imports a month.

Using the camera

Choose “Take a photo with your camera”. On a phone the camera opens; on a computer the webcam. Lay the note flat, in good light, and take the photo straight from above with the whole page in view.

What the AI does

Savour first turns the photo upright and then reads a higher-contrast copy. The AI reads what is there and invents nothing: what is not on the note is not in the recipe. When the AI is unsure, you see it in the review screen.

The original is kept

For a recipe read from a photo, the note is kept. Open the recipe and choose “View original note” to compare it with the recipe.

Several photos in a zip

If your photos are in a zip file, choose it under “From another recipe app” or “All files mixed”. If it contains only photos, Savour reads each photo as a recipe. See A whole folder or zip.

Tips for a good photo

One recipe per photo, no shadow from your phone, no flash on glossy paper. Then check the numbers in particular. More tips: From scribbled note to recipe card.

iPhone photos (HEIC)

Savour reads .jpg, .png and .webp. If your iPhone saves photos as HEIC, choose “Most Compatible” under Settings → Camera → Formats, or use the camera inside Savour.
