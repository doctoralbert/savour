Organise recipes the way your kitchen works

One recipe book for everything you have ever made, arranged your way.

  • Your structureFolders and categories the way your kitchen works.
  • Find what you rememberSearch titles, ingredients and method.
  • Calm during serviceA list, a star for favourites and one tap to the recipe.

Your structure

Sauces, stocks, garnishes, pastry, this season’s tasting menu. You create the folders and categories. A recipe can live in several folders, so your jus belongs to both the stocks and the lamb course.

Search by what you remember

Only remember there was yuzu in it? Savour searches titles, ingredients and method. Or browse your whole book from A to Z.

Calm and quick

No crowded screens. A list of titles, a star for your favourites and one tap to the recipe. Made for looking something up in the middle of service.

On every device

Organise and import on the computer, use your phone or tablet in the kitchen with large text in cook mode. Nothing to install.

Do not start with a hundred folders

Too many folders work as badly as none. Start with the structure everyone in the kitchen already knows, such as your sections, and only add something when you notice it is missing. A good test: can a new cook find the hollandaise within a minute without asking?

Linking base recipes

Many dishes lean on the same stocks and sauces. Link an ingredient to its base recipe and it is one click away. Add a short note if you like, for example about shelf life or the supplier you use. That keeps the method itself clear.

Folders per section, menu or season

Create folders yourself and give them the names your kitchen uses. A recipe can be in several folders without existing twice.

Everything in one place

All recipes in a row, with search and A to Z. Star what you make every week.

Frequently asked questions

Can a recipe be in several folders?

Yes. Your veal jus can belong to the basics and to the lamb menu, and still be one recipe.

What does Savour search?

The title, the ingredients and the method. Or you browse from A to Z.

What do I do with old menus?

Put them in an archive folder. They are out of sight, but still searchable.
