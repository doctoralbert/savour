Sharing and kitchen teams

Send one recipe quickly, or keep one recipe book for your whole brigade.

On this pageSharing one recipeA kitchen teamEverything about kitchen teamsWhat it costsRead more

Sharing one recipe

Send a recipe as a link, even to someone without an account, and decide how long the link works. See Sharing a recipe with a link.

A kitchen team

A kitchen team is your kitchen’s recipe book. Every member sees the same recipes on their own phone, tablet or computer. When the maker changes a recipe, everyone sees the new version straight away.
