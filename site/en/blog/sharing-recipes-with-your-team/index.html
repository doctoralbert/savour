Sharing recipes with your team, without photos of photos

A photo of a recipe, forwarded in the group chat, photographed again from another screen. Sounds familiar. And nobody knows which version is right.

23 October 2026

Share a whole folder

Create a shared folder, for example “Saucier” or “Autumn menu”, and invite your colleagues. Everyone sees the same recipes, and a change shows up for everyone straight away.

Choose what each person may do

There are three roles. The owner manages the folder. An editor can add and change recipes. A viewer can read everything but change nothing. You decide who touches the recipes.

Send a single recipe

Want to share one recipe, for example with a colleague at another restaurant or a supplier? Share it with a link. The recipient sees the recipe neatly, without an account.

Colleagues who build along

A shared folder does not have to come from you alone. Give your sous-chef edit rights and let the team add recipes themselves. The recipe book grows with the kitchen.

What it costs

Being a member of a shared folder is free. Creating a shared folder yourself is part of Savour Pro. With a team plan everyone in your kitchen gets Pro.

Shared folders with roles are under “Shared folders” in Savour.
