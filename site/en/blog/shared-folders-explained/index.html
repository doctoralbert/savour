Kitchen teams: how your shared recipe book works

A kitchen team is the heart of Savour: one recipe book for your whole kitchen, on everyone’s phone, always the same version. This is everything you can do with it.

25 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • One recipe book for your whole kitchen, on everyone’s phone and always the same version.
  • The owner manages, a sous-chef adds and edits, a cook views, prints and converts.
  • Being a member is free; creating a kitchen team needs Savour Pro.

One recipe book for your team

Everyone who is a member of a kitchen team sees the same recipes on their own phone, tablet or computer. When the creator changes a recipe, the whole team sees the new version straight away. No loose copies, no old printouts on the wall.

Roles: owner, sous-chef and cook

The owner manages the folder, the members and their roles. A sous-chef adds recipes and folders, removes them and manages the folders. A cook can view, print and scale. Only the creator of a recipe can edit it, so nobody changes someone else’s recipe by accident.

RoleCan doFits
Ownermanages the kitchen team, the members and their rolethe head chef
Sous-chefadds recipes and folders and edits themsous-chef and chefs de partie

Adding recipes

Choose “Add recipe” and tick recipes from your own library. The recipe stays yours: it is in the kitchen team and in your own recipes at the same time.

Folders per section or per menu

Split the kitchen team into folders: one per section (garde manger, saucier, pastry) or one per menu. With “Manage folders” you rename them, put them in your kitchen’s order and archive folders you no longer use, like last season’s menu. Archived folders and their recipes are kept under “Archive”.

Inviting your team

Invite colleagues via WhatsApp, Telegram, email or the invitation link. When you invite someone, you choose whether they become a sous-chef or a cook; as the owner you can always change that later. Being a member of a kitchen team is free, also without Savour Pro.

Printing for the binder

Print the whole kitchen team, one folder or your own selection in one go. You get a table of contents, the number of each recipe at the bottom of the page so you can leaf through quickly, and with Savour Pro your restaurant’s logo at the top.

Scaling and cook mode

Scale any recipe to the yield you need. Cook mode shows only the recipe in large type and keeps your screen on; tick off ingredients and steps as you work.

Copying a recipe

On every colleague’s recipe in the kitchen team you see “Add to my recipes”. You get your own copy to change, while the original stays with its creator.

Also without internet

Recipes you have opened stay available on your phone. Even in a basement kitchen without wifi you can read them.

Leaving or deleting

A member can always leave a kitchen team. Only the owner can delete it; the recipes themselves simply stay in their creator’s library.

Frequently asked questions

A few questions chefs often ask us about kitchen teams.

Does every member need Savour Pro?

No. Being a member of a kitchen team is free. Only the person who creates a kitchen team needs Pro.

What happens if the owner stops Pro?

Then the kitchen team is locked, but everything is kept: recipes, folders and members. As soon as the owner has Pro again, the team opens straight away.

Do the recipes of someone who leaves stay?

Recipes in the kitchen team stay with the kitchen team, even when someone leaves.

You create a kitchen team with Savour Pro; anyone can be a member for free. In every kitchen team you also find this explanation behind the i button.
