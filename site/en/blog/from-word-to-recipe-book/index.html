From Word document to recipe book in a few minutes

Many chefs already have it: one big Word file with years of recipes. Retyping takes weeks. It does not have to.

23 October 2026

Drop the file in

Choose your Word file or PDF in Savour. The Savour reader goes through the whole document and splits it into separate recipes itself, even if there are hundreds.

What the reader recognises

Titles, also when bold or numbered, ingredients with quantity and unit, and the method in steps. Numbers before titles and small typos, such as 18oc instead of 180°C, are cleaned up.

When the reader is unsure

Not every document is neatly formatted. If the reader is unsure about a recipe, it is flagged and you check it yourself before it is saved. With Savour Pro the AI reads those doubtful cases automatically.

How fast it is

One chef imported 1,400 recipes from Word this way in about two minutes. Then you can put them in folders, complete them and share them straight away.

Free or Pro

You import Word, PDF and text for free with the Savour reader, up to 100 recipes. With Savour Pro you store unlimited recipes and the AI also reads photos and handwritten notes.

You find importing in Savour under “Add recipe”.
