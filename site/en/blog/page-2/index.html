Features

Everything Savour does for you in the kitchen, from the first note to the print on the wall.

  1. 01Digitise recipesWord, PDF and photos of handwritten recipes into Savour in one go.
  2. 02Organise recipesFolders, categories and search by ingredient. Your beurre blanc in three seconds.
  3. 03Share with your brigadeKitchen teams with rights: you manage, your sous-chef edits, the rest can view.
  4. 04Scale recipesFrom 8 covers to 120, without calculation errors.
  5. 05Printing recipesA single recipe or a complete recipe folder in one go, neatly printed for the kitchen.
  6. 06Recipe database and archiveAll your recipes safe in the cloud, online and offline on every device.
  7. 07Import serviceWe move your whole recipe folder over for you, checked recipe by recipe.
  8. 08Coming soonComing soonCost per recipe, allergens and the history of every recipe.
