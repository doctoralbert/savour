Having your recipe folder moved over: why kitchens put it off and how it does get done

Almost every chef wants their recipes digital. Almost nobody has the time to move them over. That is why the chefs behind Savour do it for you.

27 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • The cooks behind Savour read in your whole recipe folder and check every recipe against the original.
  • You choose the folder structure; nothing is ever invented.
  • On day one your brigade opens the same recipe book.

Why it keeps waiting

Moving a recipe folder over is not a job for between services. Hundreds of recipes spread across Word files, notebooks and photos, and every recipe has to be copied, checked and given a place. So the folder waits, until someone leaves or the folder is lost.

What goes wrong when you do it halfway

Letting an intern retype everything leads to typos in quantities. A scanner without checks quietly turns 1 tbsp into 1 l. Going digital halfway is worse than not at all: nobody trusts the app any more and the paper binder comes back to the pass.

You choose the structure

You choose a package and request the import service. Once approved, you pay and send us everything: Word, PDF, photos of notebooks and notes, or exports from another app. You tell us your own folder structure, by section, by type or by menu, exactly the way your kitchen works.

  1. Choose a package and requestYou choose how many recipes and for which team, and request the service.
  2. Approval and paymentAfter approval you pay and get a link to hand in your files.
  3. Hand inWord, PDF, photos of notebooks and notes or exports, with your structure.
  4. Read in and checkedWe read everything in and check every recipe against the original.
  5. Your team is readyEveryone gets an account and an email to choose a password.

Every recipe checked

We read everything with the Savour reader and lay every recipe next to the original: quantities, units, yield and method. Where the source leaves doubt, we look extra carefully. Nothing is ever made up; what is not there, we do not fill in.

Everything ready for your brigade

You give us the email addresses of your colleagues. Everyone gets an account with Savour Pro, access to the recipes and an email to choose their own password. On day one your whole brigade opens the same recipe book, on the phone, the tablet at the pass and the office computer.

Your recipes stay yours

The files you send are kept in private storage that only Savour can see, and are deleted as soon as your team is ready. After that your recipes only live in your team.

What it costs

One-off €85 up to 100 recipes, €100 up to 500 and €164.99 up to 1,000, together with a yearly plan for your team of 5, 10 or 25 people. More recipes by arrangement. The first five kitchens are imported for free.

5kitchens we load in for free. After that the package that fits your folder applies.

What you still do yourself

Three things: hand in the files, tell us your folder structure and send the email addresses of your team. When everything is ready, check a few recipes you know well. After that the paper folder no longer lies at the pass, but in the drawer.
