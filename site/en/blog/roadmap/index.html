Roadmap: what we are working on

Savour is built by a chef, for chefs. That also means we build what the kitchen really needs, not what sounds good in a presentation.

23 October 2026

Cost per recipe

See what a dish and a portion cost, and what happens when the price of a product goes up. This is the most requested feature and sits at the top.

Allergens at a glance

Per recipe and per composed dish, so you can answer a guest’s question at the table without walking into the kitchen.

History of every recipe

See who changed what in a shared recipe, and restore an earlier version when needed. Printing with your own logo is already done and part of Savour Pro.

An app for iPhone and Android

Savour now works in the browser on computer, tablet and phone, without installing anything. We are considering a separate app. Whether it is worth the investment depends on what chefs think.

How we choose

We look at what chefs tell us and at what saves the most time in the kitchen. Have an idea or missing something? Email us at contact@savour.page. We read everything.

This roadmap is a direction, not a promise with dates. We keep it up to date here.
