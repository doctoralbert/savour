A recipe book with your restaurant’s logo

Even with every recipe on your phone, paper still matters in many kitchens. Then it may as well look like your restaurant.

24 October 2026 · 2 min read

In short

  • With Savour Pro your restaurant’s logo and name are at the top of every printed page.
  • In a team everyone prints in the same style, with the owner’s logo.
  • A whole folder becomes a book: cover page, table of contents and the print date at the bottom.

Your logo and name on every page

With Savour Pro you set your restaurant’s logo and name once. After that they appear at the top of every printed page: the logo on the left, the name in the centre. For kitchen printers you can print the logo in black and white.

  1. Open settingsGo to Settings → Logo on prints.
  2. Logo and nameChoose the logo and fill in your restaurant’s name.
  3. Black and white?Printing on a kitchen printer? Let the logo print in black and white.
  4. PrintFrom now on your logo is on every recipe and folder you print.

For your whole team

With a team plan the owner’s logo applies to everyone in the team. So everyone prints in the same style, whoever prints. In a kitchen team the logo of the restaurant that manages the folder is used.

A whole folder as a book

When you print a folder, you can add a cover page with the logo, the folder name and a table of contents. Choose A4 for the kitchen binder or A5 for a small folder at the pass.

A4
  • the binder in the kitchen
  • many recipes per folder
  • easy to read from a distance
A5
  • a small folder at the pass
  • handy on a narrow station
  • fits in an apron pocket or drawer

Always clear whether it is current

The print date is at the bottom of every page, so everyone in the kitchen can see whether a binder is still the latest version. The browser’s headers and footers are left out, so the page stays calm.

Choose what you print

In a kitchen team you choose which recipes to print before printing, grouped by folder. Only what that section or that event needs.

What is on a printed page

At the top the logo on the left and your restaurant’s name in the middle. Below that the recipe itself, calmly laid out. At the bottom the print date and, in a folder, the recipe number, so you can leaf through quickly. The browser’s header and footer are left out.

Print recipesOne recipe, a folder or your own selection at once.→

Printing with your own logo is part of Savour Pro. You set it up under Settings → Subscription and team.
