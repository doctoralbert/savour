Sorting out a messy recipe folder with AI, then printing it in one go

Years of recipes in one binder: typed, scanned, handwritten and full of numbers. Retyping takes weeks. With the right help it takes an afternoon.

26 October 2026 · 2 min read

In short

  • Put the whole folder into Savour at once; the reader splits and tidies up.
  • What the reader is unsure of, it marks. The AI reads notes and doubtful cases (Pro).
  • Then folders per section, menu or season, and if needed print the whole folder.

Where it usually starts

A binder with recipes from different chefs, with duplicate titles, loose headings and a recipe that runs on halfway down a page. Nobody knows which version is right any more, and searching takes longer than cooking.

Let the reader do the heavy lifting

Put the whole file into Savour at once. The Savour reader splits it into separate recipes, removes numbers before titles, recognises parts such as dough and filling and corrects common spelling mistakes. Whatever it is unsure about, it flags.

  1. Everything inThe whole Word file, the PDF or the pile of photos.
  2. Let it splitThe reader turns it into separate recipes and removes numbers and loose headings.
  3. Check onceMarked recipes first; each recipe has a suggested category.
  4. OrganiseFolders per section, menu or season. A recipe can be in several folders.
  5. Print when neededThe complete folder for the binder, with a table of contents.

AI for notes and doubtful cases

Photos of handwritten notes and recipes the reader is unsure about are read by the AI (Savour Pro). Important: a good AI never invents quantities. Anything unreadable is marked for you to check.

Review once, then save

You see all recipes in one overview, each with a suggested category. Fix what is wrong and save everything at once.

Organise in folders

Create folders per section, menu or season. A recipe can sit in several folders without existing twice. Share a folder with your brigade and everyone works from the same version.

Print the complete folder at once

Does the new folder need to go in the binder at the pass? Print the complete recipe folder at once, with a table of contents and a clean layout. With Savour Pro your restaurant’s logo is on every page.

Which version is right?

An old folder sometimes holds the same sauce two or three times, from different chefs. Decide with your sous-chef which version the kitchen makes now, and give the others a clear name or put them in the archive. Then nobody searches between three almost identical recipes again.

No rush to organise

Recipes you have not put in a folder yet are still easy to find via search and A to Z. So organising can wait for a quiet moment after service.

In Savour you import a whole recipe folder at once, organise everything in folders and print a complete folder with one click.
