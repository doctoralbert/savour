Savour for pastry chefs and bakers

In the kitchen a sauce may turn out a little different; in pastry it may not. There a recipe is a formula, and it has to be right every time. This is how Savour helps, from the old notebook to today’s batch.

SAVOUR

20 November 2026 · by Jop Schreurs · 3 min read

In short

  • Your recipes stay to the gram exactly as you wrote them; Savour never invents anything.
  • Scale by pieces, by yield or by what you have left, without a calculator.
  • Base recipes such as crème pâtissière count in the allergens of every pastry.

First bring your notebooks over

Most pastry chefs keep their recipes in a notebook, a binder of loose sheets or a Word file. Savour reads Word, PDF and text for free, even with hundreds of recipes in one file. Photos of handwritten recipes are read by the AI, with Savour Pro. If Savour is unsure about something, you see it before anything is saved.

  1. Choose Add recipe → ImportWord, PDF, text or a photo of your notebook.
  2. Check itEvery recipe stands on its own, with ingredients and method; doubts are yellow.
  3. Save and sortFor example a folder per kind: doughs, creams, ice cream, glazes, viennoiserie.

Scaling by pieces

A recipe for 24 financiers, and tomorrow you need 480 for an event? Enter the yield and Savour converts everything: 3 kg of butter, 3.6 kg of egg white. Grams become kilos once it is more than 10 kilos, and the original recipe always stays as it is.

Or by what you have

Still 3 kg of chocolate on the shelf that you want to use up? Choose ⋯ More scaling options, enter 3 kg of chocolate under "I have", and Savour scales the whole recipe to that amount. A factor of your own works too, such as 2.5×.

Parts keep it readable

Put headings like "For the dough:" and "For the filling:" between your ingredients. Savour keeps them apart, also when you scale or print.

Allergens, also through your base recipes

A crème pâtissière, a sweet pastry or a glaze ends up in ten different pastries. Link them as base recipes and their allergens count in every pastry made with them. Savour looks up the allergens of every ingredient: flour is gluten, egg white is egg, butter is milk, almond flour is nuts.

Chocolate differs by brand

Chocolate, couverture and similar products are marked "may differ by brand". Savour never says something is allergen-free while not everything is checked; your supplier’s label comes first.

Printing for the bakery

Print a single recipe or a whole folder at once, scaled if you like, with or without allergens. With Savour Pro your business logo is on it. And what has to be ready today goes into a prep list your team ticks off.

What Savour does not do (yet)

Savour cannot work with baker’s percentages, and it cannot calculate a cost per pastry yet; that is on Coming soon. You scale by yield, by pieces, by a factor or by an ingredient you have.

What does it cost?

Free up to 100 recipes. Savour Pro costs €2.99 per month or €29 per year, with unlimited recipes, allergens, AI for photos and printing with your logo. New accounts get 7 days of Pro for free. More on For pastry chefs and bakers.
