A recipe database for your kitchen: online, offline and in the cloud

Most kitchens keep their recipes in ten places at once. A recipe database brings that back to one place that you can open anywhere.

26 October 2026 · 2 min read

In short

  • One place for all recipes, searchable and in the same format.
  • In the cloud your database is the same on every device, and a change shows everywhere at once.
  • Choose an app that works without internet, and archive instead of throwing away.

The problem: recipes in ten places

Word files on the previous chef’s laptop, photos on three phones, a stained binder at the pass. Each of those places can disappear: a broken phone, a colleague who leaves, a binder that ends up in the dish room.

What a good recipe database does

All recipes in one place, searchable by title, ingredient and method. Every recipe in the same shape: title, yield, ingredients and steps. And with folders and categories, so you do not have to scroll through hundreds of recipes.

What a good recipe database does
  • Every recipe in the same format: title, yield, ingredients, method
  • Search by title, ingredient and method
  • Folders and categories
  • The same on computer, tablet and phone
  • Opens without internet too
  • Your recipes stay yours when you stop paying

Why in the cloud

When your database is in the cloud, it is the same everywhere: on the office computer, on the tablet at the pass and on your phone at home. A change is visible everywhere at once, and a new phone just means logging in again.

And offline in the kitchen

Kitchens are bad for wifi: steel, walk-ins, basements. So choose a recipe app that also works without a connection. In Savour you open your recipes in the walk-in or at an event, once you have opened the app online.

An archive, not a bin

Do not throw old menus away. An archive of past seasons is gold when a dish comes back or a guest asks about an old dessert. Put them in an archive folder: out of sight, but always findable.

Searching the way a cook searches

A cook does not search by file name, but by what is in their head: “that sauce with tarragon”, “something with rhubarb”. That is why Savour also searches ingredients and method, not just titles. And if you prefer to browse, pick a letter in A to Z.

A star for the regulars

Star the recipes you make every week. Then they are one tap away in your favourites, without searching.

Savour is your recipe database and recipe archive in the cloud, on computer, tablet and phone, and available offline.
