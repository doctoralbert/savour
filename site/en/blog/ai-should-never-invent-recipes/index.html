Why AI should never invent your recipes

AI is good at filling in what is probably there. In a kitchen, “probably” is not good enough.

23 October 2026

The problem with guessing

Read a blurry photo of a note and you might guess it says 150 grams of sugar. But if it was 150 grams of glucose, your marshmallow is no longer right. A smart guess looks just as convincing as the right answer.

What Savour does differently

Savour never invents quantities, ingredients or steps. If the method is not on the note, it is not in the recipe either. You add it yourself.

Making doubt visible

When the reader or the AI is not sure, you see it. The recipe is flagged and you check it before it is saved. Better a question to you than a mistake on the plate.

The chef decides

AI is a tool to work faster, not a replacement for craft. The last word on a recipe always belongs to the cook who made it.

This is one of Savour’s core values: a recipe has to be right.
