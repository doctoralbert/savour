Printing and downloading

A single recipe, a whole recipe folder for the pass or all your recipes, with your restaurant’s logo. And downloading your recipes as Word or a backup.

On this pagePrinting a recipePrinting several recipes or a folderPrinting with your restaurant’s logoDownloading recipes: Word and backup

Printing a recipe

A single recipe for the wall or the binder at the pass.

Printing

Open the recipe and choose “Print”. Savour opens the print layout: the name, the yield, the ingredients with bold quantities and the method in steps. Then choose your printer, or save it as a PDF.

Printing scaled

If you have scaled the recipe, you print the scaled quantities. Handy for a large production or an event.

Parts of the recipe

If the recipe has parts, each part appears with its name above its own ingredients and method.

More recipes at once

Print a whole folder, a kitchen team or your own selection in one go: see Printing several recipes.

With your logo

With Savour Pro your restaurant’s logo is on your prints. See Printing with your logo.

Printing several recipes or a folder

Your complete recipe folder for the binder at the pass, in one go.

What you can print

Print a recipe folder, a kitchen team or all your recipes at once. Print all your recipes under My account → Account with “Print all recipes”.

Choosing recipes

At “Which recipes do you want to print?” you tick which recipes go in. “All” or “None” gives you a quick start.

Options

Choose how the print looks:

  • “With cover page and contents”: a cover page, a table of contents and each recipe’s number at the bottom of the page, so you can flick through quickly.
  • “A5, for a small binder at the pass” instead of A4.
  • “With photos”: each recipe’s photo.
  • “With notes”: your notes on each recipe.

A tip for the pass

Print on A5 for a small ring binder or sleeves. When a recipe changes, reprint just that recipe.

Printing with your restaurant’s logo

Your own recipe book, with your own name and logo on it.

Setting it up

Go to My account → Logo on prints. Choose your logo and enter the name that goes on the print. This works with Savour Pro, including the trial week.

Black and white

Choose “Print logo in black and white” for a kitchen printer or if your colour logo is too busy on paper.

Your own prints and kitchen teams

With “Logo on my own prints” you choose whether the logo appears on recipes and folders outside a kitchen team. Under Kitchen teams you turn the logo on or off for each kitchen team; you can also do this in the kitchen team’s settings. In a kitchen team the owner’s logo is on the prints, so the whole team has the same prints.

Read more

Why your own recipe book with a logo works: Recipe book with your logo.

Downloading recipes: Word and backup

Your recipes are yours. Download them whenever you like.

Where

Go to My account → Account, under “Print or download all your recipes”. Downloading works with Savour Pro.

Three ways

Choose what you need:

  • “Word per recipe”: each recipe its own Word file in the print layout, together in a .zip.
  • “Everything in one Word file”: a simple layout with title, yield, ingredients and method.
  • “Backup”: recipes and folders as a JSON file.

Restoring a backup

A backup can be imported into Savour again under “Other files”. See JSON and backups.

Without Pro

Without Pro your recipes always stay in Savour, and you can view and print them. Stopping Pro never costs you recipes: see Stopping Savour Pro.
