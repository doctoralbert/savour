Getting started with Savour

What Savour is, who it is for and the first steps to your own digital recipe book.

On this pageWhat is SavourWho it is forStart in four stepsFree or Savour ProOn every device

What is Savour

Savour is a recipe app for professional kitchens. You bring all your recipes together in one place: from Word, PDF, photos of handwritten notes, websites and other recipe apps. Then you organise them in folders and categories, scale them to any yield, print them for the binder at the pass and share them with your brigade in a kitchen team.

Who it is for

For head chefs, sous-chefs, pastry chefs, caterers and culinary schools: anyone who needs to keep, share and quickly find recipes. Home cooks can use Savour too, but the app is built for kitchen work: large quantities, standard recipes and a team working from the same version. Read more at For chefs and For kitchen brigades.

Start in four steps

This gets your first recipes into Savour within fifteen minutes:

  • Create an account, or look around the demo first without an account.
  • Import your recipes: one Word file or PDF with all your recipes is enough to start.
  • Check what was read and save.
  • Put Savour on your phone as an app, so your recipes are always at hand in the kitchen.

Free or Savour Pro

A free account keeps up to 100 recipes and imports Word, PDF and text. With Savour Pro you keep unlimited recipes, AI reads photos of handwritten notes, you create kitchen teams and you print with your restaurant’s logo. New accounts get 7 days of Pro for free.

On every device

Savour runs in the browser on computer, tablet and phone. Importing, organising and printing are easiest on a computer; looking things up in the kitchen on a phone or tablet. Your recipes live in the cloud: on a new device you log in and everything is there.
