Working without internet

No wifi in the basement kitchen? Your recipes still open.

On this pageHow it worksMaking sure it worksWhat works without internetWhat needs internetRead more

How it works

Savour keeps recipes you have opened on your device. When the connection drops, Savour opens your library from that device.

Making sure it works

Log in once with “Stay signed in” ticked and open the recipes you need in the kitchen. Preferably put Savour on your home screen as an app.

What works without internet

Reading, searching your library, scaling and cook mode work as normal.

What needs internet

Saving, editing, importing and sharing work again as soon as you have a connection.
