Using categories and folders well

What a recipe is, and how you work with it: two different things.

On this pageCategory: what it isFolder: how you workNamesRead more

Category: what it is

Use categories for what a recipe is, such as sauces, garnishes or pastry. A recipe is in one category.

Folder: how you work

Use folders for how you work, such as the current menu, an event or the cold kitchen. A recipe can appear in several folders.

Names

Keep names short and recognisable, so everyone in the team finds the recipe with one search word.
