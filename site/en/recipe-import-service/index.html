We import your whole recipe folder for you

No time to move hundreds of recipes over? The founder of Savour, a chef, does it for you personally. You hand over your folder, your team gets a recipe book that is right.

Why we do this

The biggest hurdle for a kitchen is not the app, it is moving everything over. Three hundred recipes in Word, a stained binder, notebooks from the previous chef: nobody has time for that next to service. So we do it.

By a chef, not a call centre

Your recipes are imported by someone who has worked in a kitchen. Someone who knows what a stock is, that 1 tbsp is not 1 litre and that you want to find your mise en place by section. Every recipe is laid next to the original and checked.

Your recipes stay yours

Recipes are the capital of your kitchen. We only use them to import them for you, share them with no one and delete your source files as soon as everything is ready.

Launch offer: the first 5 kitchens are imported for freeYou only pay for your team plan. In return we ask for honest feedback.
Have your recipes imported

What you get

  • All your recipes imported: Word, PDF, photos of notebooks and notes, and exports from other apps
  • A folder structure that matches how you work: by section, menu, season or location
  • Every recipe checked against the original: quantities, units, yield and method
  • A shared folder for your team, everyone invited, with a short walkthrough
  • Confidential: your source files are deleted afterwards

How it works

  1. RequestFill in the form. We get in touch to agree how many recipes there are and how you want them organised.
  2. Send your recipesSend your files, photos or exports. However messy: that is what Savour was made for.
  3. Import and checkWe import everything, organise it in folders and check every recipe.
  4. You approveYou go through it all, your team gets an invitation and you can start cooking.
