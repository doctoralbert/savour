Your first week with Savour

A practical plan to go from loose files to one recipe book in a week.

On this pageDay 1: get everything inDay 2: reviewDay 3: tidy upDay 4: organiseDay 5: bring in the teamDays 6 and 7: in the kitchen

Day 1: get everything in

Collect your Word files, PDFs and old exports and import them in one go. If you have handwritten notes, take photos of them during the Savour Pro trial week.

Day 2: review

Go through the recipes Savour marked for review. Compare doubtful ones with the original and check the numbers in particular. See Reviewing an import.

Day 3: tidy up

Remove numbers in front of recipe names and duplicate recipes with Clean up. Then add the default category list and let Savour suggest categories.

Day 4: organise

Create folders the way your kitchen works: by section, by menu or by season. Give your most used recipes a star.

Day 5: bring in the team

Create a kitchen team, add the base recipes and invite your team. Give your sous-chef the editor role.

Days 6 and 7: in the kitchen

Put Savour on the phones as an app and on the tablet at the pass, use cook mode during mise en place and print the binder for the pass.
