Creating and editing a recipe

Entering a new recipe or changing an existing one, step by step.

On this pageA new recipeThe fieldsA recipe in partsSavingEditing a recipeWho can editMany recipes quickly

A new recipe

Choose “Add recipe” in the menu and then “Enter manually”. The “New recipe” screen opens with the fields Name, Yield, Category, Recipe photo, Ingredients and Method.

The fields

How to fill them in:

  • Name: short and recognisable, so everyone finds the recipe with one search word.
  • Yield: what the recipe makes, such as “1 litre” or “40 pieces”.
  • Category: what kind of recipe it is, such as sauces or pastry.
  • Photo: optional, a photo of the dish.
  • Ingredients: each ingredient on its own line.
  • Method: short steps, one action per step.

A recipe in parts

Does your recipe have dough, filling and sauce? Choose “＋ Add part” and give each part its own ingredients and method. See A recipe in parts.

Saving

Choose “Save”. “Cancel” or “← Back” returns without saving. If an identical recipe already exists, it is not saved twice.

Editing a recipe

Open the recipe and choose “Edit”. You see the same fields. Change what you want and choose “Save”. Members of your kitchen team see the new version straight away.

Who can edit

Only the maker of a recipe can edit it. In a kitchen team others can see the recipe; with “Add to my recipes” they take their own copy to change as they like. See Copying a recipe.

Many recipes quickly

If your recipes are already in Word, PDF or another app, importing is much faster than retyping.
