Home / Features / Recipe database and archive

Your recipe database, online and offline on every device

One recipe archive in the cloud for everything your kitchen has ever made. On the computer, the tablet at the pass and the phone in your apron.

Everything stored in the cloud

Every recipe you import or write is stored safely in the cloud. If your phone ends up in the fryer or you move to another restaurant, your recipe book comes with you. Sign in on another device and everything is there.

The same on every device

Import and organise on the laptop, read on the tablet at the pass and search on your phone in the walk-in. What you change on one device shows up on the other too. Nothing to get from an app store: you add Savour to your home screen.

Offline in the kitchen

No wifi in the basement or the walk-in? Savour keeps your library on your device. Without a connection you simply open your recipes to read them. As soon as you are back online, Savour fetches the latest version.

An archive that lasts

Old menus, last season’s recipes and the notebook of your first head chef: everything stays stored and easy to find. If you stop Savour Pro, you keep all your recipes. Savour never deletes anything by itself.
