Leaving, deleting and Savour Pro

What happens to the recipes when someone leaves or stops.

Leaving

A member can always leave a kitchen team. You keep your own recipes; you no longer see other people’s. To keep one, copy it first.

Deleting

Only the owner can delete the kitchen team, under General in the settings. The recipes themselves stay in their makers’ libraries.

When the owner stops Pro

A kitchen team works as long as the owner has Savour Pro, including the trial week. If that stops, the team is locked, but everything is kept until the owner has Pro again.
