How does importing work?

Everyone uses the Savour reader: it reads Word, PDF and text instantly and for free. With Savour Pro the AI also reads what the reader is unsure about: photos, handwritten notes, scanned pages and documents without a clear layout. The AI never invents quantities or steps; you check every recipe before it is saved.

Questions

What happens if I stop Pro?
You keep all your recipes and kitchen teams. You just cannot add new recipes beyond 100 or create new kitchen teams. A kitchen team is kept, but can only be used again once the owner has Savour Pro.

How do the members of my organisation get Pro?
You add them via My team in the menu, or on your phone via My account → Savour Pro. They get an invitation by email and have Savour Pro as soon as they log in or sign up with exactly that email address. You count as the first seat yourself.

Do my members have to pay too?
No. Being a member of a kitchen team is free. If you want everyone in your kitchen to have Pro, choose an team subscription. A kitchen team works as long as the owner has Savour Pro. With a free account you can view, print and scale the recipes in a kitchen team; adding and editing recipes is possible with Savour Pro.

What counts as an AI import?
Every photo, scanned page or part of a file the AI reads (about 8 recipes per part). What the Savour reader reads with certainty does not count.

What if I go over 150?
Then the Savour reader carries on and you can do the rest with AI next month. There are never extra costs.

How do I cancel?
In My account → Savour Pro → Manage subscription. There you can also change your payment method and download invoices.

Rather have us import your recipes?

With the import service, the chefs behind Savour personally move your whole recipe folder over and set everything up for your team. From €85 one-off, with an team plan of your choice.
