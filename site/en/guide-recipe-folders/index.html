Recipe folders

Organise your recipes the way your kitchen works: by section, menu or season.

On this pageCreating a folderRecipes in a folderRemoving from a folderMenu and orderPrinting a folderOnly for you

Creating a folder

In the menu under Recipe folders choose “Add recipe folder” and give it a name, for example “Garde manger”, “Lunch menu” or “Christmas”.

Recipes in a folder

Open a folder and choose which recipes belong in it. Or add a recipe to a folder from the recipe itself: choose ⋯ and “Add to folder”. A recipe can be in several folders.

Removing from a folder

Open the recipe from the folder and choose ⋯ “Remove from folder”. The recipe stays in your library.

Menu and order

Choose which folders appear in the menu and drag them into the order that makes sense to you.

Printing a folder

Print a whole folder at once, with cover page, table of contents and each recipe’s number at the bottom of the page. See Printing several recipes.

Only for you

Only you see your own folders. Folders you share with your team are made in a kitchen team.
