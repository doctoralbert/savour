Free and Savour Pro

What is free, what Savour Pro adds and how the trial week works.

Free

A free account keeps up to 100 recipes, imports Word, PDF, text and exports from other apps with the Savour reader, scales, has cook mode, prints and can be a member of kitchen teams.

Savour Pro

Savour Pro costs €2.99 a month or €29 a year and adds:

  • Unlimited recipes.
  • AI for photos, handwriting and doubtful recipes, up to 500 imports a month.
  • Creating kitchen teams and inviting your team.
  • Printing with your restaurant’s logo.
  • Downloading as a Word file or backup.
  • No ads in the app.

The trial week

New accounts get 7 days of Savour Pro for free, no payment details needed. During the trial you keep up to 100 recipes.

Paying

Pay with iDEAL, credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. No VAT is charged (Dutch small business scheme). Cancel any time; you keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

For a whole kitchen

If everyone in your kitchen needs Pro, choose an organisation. All prices are on Savour Pro.

Organisations: Pro for your whole kitchen

Savour Pro for everyone in your kitchen, with one subscription.

What it is

With an organisation everyone in your team has Savour Pro. There are plans for 5, 10 or 25 people: €8.99, €14.99 or €29.99 a month.

Setting it up

Choose an organisation on the Savour Pro page, or go to My account → Savour Pro. Invite your team to the seats there.

Organisation or kitchen team

An organisation is about who has Pro. A kitchen team is about which recipes you share. For a team that shares recipes, one owner with Pro is enough; with an organisation everyone can also have photos read, create kitchen teams and print with a logo.

Managing your subscription

Everything about your subscription in one place: My account → Savour Pro.

Where

Go to My account → Savour Pro. It shows whether you have Savour Pro, a trial week or an organisation, and until when.

Getting Pro

Choose Savour Pro monthly or yearly and pay with iDEAL, credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. You have Pro right after paying.

Changes and invoices

Choose “Manage subscription” under Savour Pro. There you change your payment details, view your invoices and cancel.

Logo on prints

You set up the logo for your prints in the Logo on prints tab.

Stopping Savour Pro

You always keep everything. This is what changes.

Cancelling

Cancel via My account → Savour Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

Your recipes stay

If you stop Pro, all your recipes stay, even if you have more than 100. You can view, scale and print them. Only adding new recipes beyond 100 is no longer possible.

Your kitchen team

A kitchen team you own is locked while you have no Pro. Everything is kept; with Pro it works again. See Leaving, deleting and Savour Pro.

After the trial week

The same happens after the free trial week: everything you made stays.

My account: profile and password

Your profile and account settings in one place.

Where

Choose Settings in the menu, or on your phone the My account icon at the top. My account has the tabs Account, Savour Pro, Logo on prints, Appearance, Categories and Clean up.

Profile

Under Account you change your name and profile photo. You crop a profile photo so your face is nicely in view.

Password

Choose a new password under Account. If you no longer know your password, use “Forgot password?” when logging in.

More under Account

Here you print or download all your recipes, invite a colleague, install the app, log out and delete your account.

The other tabs

Savour Pro, Logo on prints, Appearance, Categories and Clean up.

Dark or light theme and language

Make Savour read best in your kitchen.

Dark or light

Savour is dark with gold by default. Choose “Dark” or “Light” under My account → Appearance, or switch with the button at the top. Your choice is saved in your account, so on your other devices too.

Language

The language button at the top switches Savour to English, Dutch, German, Spanish or French. The buttons and explanations change along.

Your recipes stay as they are

Recipes and names are never translated. A recipe in Dutch stays Dutch, even when you use Savour in English.

Deleting your account

Gone is gone. Download what you want to keep first.

Download first

To keep your recipes, download them first as a Word file or backup, or print them.

Deleting

Go to My account → Account and choose “Delete account permanently” at the bottom. Savour asks you to confirm first.

What is deleted

Your personal recipes, folders, favourites and profile details. This cannot be undone.

A subscription

If you have Savour Pro, cancel it first under Savour Pro.

Your recipes, privacy and security

Your recipes are yours. This is how Savour treats them.

Yours

Your recipes are yours. Savour never deletes anything without you asking, and you can always take them with you.

Who sees them

Only you see your own recipes and folders. Recipes you put in a kitchen team are seen by that team. Anyone with a shared link can open it while it works.

Where they are

Your recipes are stored in the cloud so you can reach them on any device. Recipes you have opened are also on your device for offline use.

Taking them with you

With Savour Pro you download all your recipes whenever you like.

More

Everything about which data Savour keeps and who it works with is in the privacy policy.
