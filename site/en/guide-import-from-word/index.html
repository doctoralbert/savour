Import recipes from Word

One Word document with your whole recipe book, or a hundred separate files: Savour splits them up itself.

On this pageHow to importWhat the Savour reader recognisesHow Savour spots a new recipeOld .doc filesTablesTips for the best resultPages and Google Docs

How to import

Choose “Add recipe” → “Import recipe” → “Word file”. Pick one or more .doc or .docx files and choose “Process”. Then review the recipes.

What the Savour reader recognises

The Savour reader splits a document into separate recipes on its own, even when it holds hundreds of them. For each recipe it recognises the title, yield, ingredients and method. This is free and uses no AI.

How Savour spots a new recipe

A new recipe starts at a Word heading (Heading 1), at a bold line that looks like a recipe name, or at a recipe name followed by a new “Ingredients” heading. Headings such as “Ingredients”, “Method” and “Preparation” help the reader most. A line ending in a colon, such as “Dough:”, becomes a part of the recipe.

Old .doc files

Old .doc files from Word 97–2003 are read too. If a file does not work, save it in Word as .docx and try again.

Tables

If your ingredients are in a table, each row becomes one line with the cells in order. With tables, check the order of quantity and product.

Tips for the best result

Give each recipe a clear title as a heading, put ingredients on separate lines with the quantity first and write “Method” above the steps. More tips: A recipe everyone makes the same way.

Pages and Google Docs

Savour cannot read a Pages file: export it in Pages as Word (.docx) or PDF. In Google Docs choose File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx).
