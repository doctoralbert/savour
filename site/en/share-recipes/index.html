Home / Features / Share with your brigade

Share recipes with your brigade

Everyone in the kitchen works from the same version. No more photos of photos in the group chat.

Shared folders

Create a folder for your kitchen, your section or the new menu and invite your team. What you change, everyone sees straight away.

Rights per person

You manage the folder. Your sous-chef or chef de partie can add and edit recipes. Commis and interns can view but not change anything.

New people up to speed faster

A new cook has every recipe of the section on their phone on day one, in the version you approved.

Share single recipes

Want to send one recipe, for example to a colleague at another restaurant? Share it with a link.
