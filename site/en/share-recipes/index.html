Share recipes with your brigade

Everyone in the kitchen works from the same version. No more photos of photos in the group chat.

  • One versionWhat you change, the whole team sees at once.
  • Roles per personOwner, sous-chef and cook: you decide who may do what.
  • Free for membersBeing a member of a kitchen team costs nothing.

Kitchen teams

Create a folder for your kitchen, your section or the new menu and invite your team. What you change, everyone sees straight away.

Rights per person

You manage the folder. Your sous-chef or chef de partie can add and edit recipes. Commis and interns can view but not change anything.

New people up to speed faster

A new cook has every recipe of the section on their phone on day one, in the version you approved.

Share single recipes

Want to send one recipe, for example to a colleague at another restaurant? Share it with a link.

Who gets which role?

A practical split: the head chef is the owner, the sous-chef and chefs de partie are editors, and cooks and interns view. That keeps the recipe book with the people responsible for the flavour. If someone moves to another section for a while, you only change their role and resend nothing.

Fixing a mistake

Is there a wrong quantity in a recipe everyone uses? Change it once and the whole brigade works with the right version. No more messages saying "careful, not 40 grams but 4" that always miss the wrong person. Add a short note explaining the change, so nobody drifts back to the old way later.

Your brigade on one screen

See who is in the team and with which role. As the owner you change a role with one choice, for example when someone is on another section for a while.

On everyone’s own phone

Every member opens the recipe on their own phone, in the version that is right now. Sending one recipe works with a link, even to someone without an account.

Frequently asked questions

Do all members need Savour Pro?

No. Being a member is free. Only the person who creates a kitchen team needs Savour Pro. With a team subscription everyone in the team gets Pro.

How do I invite someone?

Via WhatsApp, Telegram, email or the invitation link. When you invite someone, you choose whether they become a sous-chef or a cook.

What happens when someone leaves?

You remove them from the team. The recipes in the kitchen team stay with the kitchen.
