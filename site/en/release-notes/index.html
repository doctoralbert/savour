Features

Everything Savour does for you in the kitchen, from the first note to the print on the wall.

  1. 01Digitise recipesWord, PDF and photos of handwritten recipes into Savour in one go.
  2. 02Organise recipesFolders, categories and search by ingredient. Your beurre blanc in three seconds.
  3. 03Share with your brigadeKitchen teams with rights: you manage, members with Pro edit, the rest view for free.
  4. 04Scale recipesFrom 8 covers to 120, without calculation errors.
  5. 05Printing recipesA single recipe or a complete recipe folder in one go, neatly printed for the kitchen.
  6. 06Allergens per recipeThe 14 allergens on every recipe, including through base recipes.
  7. 07Prep listsWhat has to be ready per section, ticked off with your brigade and calculated from covers.
  8. 08Recipe database and archiveAll your recipes safe in the cloud, online and offline on every device.
  9. 09Import serviceWe move your whole recipe folder over for you, checked recipe by recipe.

Release notes

What’s new in each version of Savour. The latest version is at the top.

SAVOUR

Version 3.11.0

20 November 2026

  • For bartenders and baristas: scaling knows dashes, barspoons and oz; oz in a drink stays oz. Cocktails go under Drinks.
  • Scaling knows singular and plural: "1 shallot" becomes "2 shallots", also in the yield.
  • Gluten in flour from German, French, Spanish and Italian recipes, such as Mehl, farine, harina and farina.
  • Soy milk, oat milk and other plant-based milks no longer get milk as an allergen.
  • New pages on the website for pastry chefs and bakers, caterers, and bartenders and baristas.

Version 3.10.0

19 November 2026

  • Prep lists (Tools → Prep lists): cards, headings and items per section, with covers per card. The brigade ticks off live, also offline, and you print the list on one A4 page, portrait or landscape. Creating and managing with Pro, ticking off for everyone.
  • Import prep lists from Excel, Word, text or a photo; you approve everything before it is saved.
  • Savour now fills in the allergens itself: whatever Savour knows counts right away, also in another language or in a derived form. Brand products show "may differ by brand". Under To check you only see what Savour does not know.
  • Duplicate recipes when importing: you see which recipes are already there and choose skip, only the changed ones, or save anyway.
  • Savour learns from what cooks correct, only when "Help improve the Savour reader" is on. Nothing changes by itself.
  • Allergen letters after ingredients ("Eier C") are removed when importing; with "Keep the letters" you keep them. A recipe across two photos becomes one recipe.
  • Save a copy of a recipe from a kitchen team in your own recipe book (Pro).
  • When creating an account you see the terms and the privacy policy, and savour.page/demo opens the demo right away.
  • Savour starts faster.

Version 3.9.0

11 November 2026

  • Community (My Savour → Community): a forum for every account, with the name from your profile, never your email.
  • Importing never loses anything: every file and every AI result is kept, and whatever is not saved yet Savour offers again.
  • The reader understands text in ChatGPT formatting, also when all ingredients are on one line.
  • The quantity goes first: "Butter 250 g" becomes "250 g butter", so scaling works.
  • Kitchen teams: viewing, printing and scaling are free; adding and editing need Savour Pro.
  • Savour Pro: up to 150 AI imports per month.
  • A notice when a new version of Savour is ready.

Version 3.8.0

8 November 2026

  • Allergens per recipe, including through base recipes. Savour recognises the ingredients from the Savour list; you only answer the questions under To check.
  • The 14 allergens by default; extra characteristics such as lactose or pork you switch on yourself.
  • An allergen chart per recipe, and when printing you choose whether the allergens go on it.
  • Scale with ½×, 1×, 2×, 5×, 10× or your own factor; the yield follows along.
  • Every recipe has its own address and the recipe name in the browser tab.
  • Default categories in your own language, and the demo fully in English, German, Spanish and French.
  • Phone: one menu at the top right with everything in one place (your account, search, recipes, folders, kitchen teams and Tools); the bar at the bottom now has four buttons.
  • The allergen chart shows the allergens in full instead of abbreviated.
  • Logging in is faster: your recipes appear right away, the Savour list loads afterwards.
  • The website now has a light mode too, with the app screens in light, and a new app icon.
  • No more ads: not in the app and not on the website, including the free version.
