Scale recipes to any yield

From a tasting for 8 to a dinner for 120, without a calculator and without mistakes.

Convert in one go

Enter the yield you need and Savour converts every quantity. The original recipe stays as it is.

Understands units

Savour knows that 3 teaspoons make a tablespoon and 1000 grams a kilo. 20 teaspoons become 6 ⅔ tablespoons, and spoons and cups stay clean fractions instead of long decimals. Even without a yield, scale with one tap: ½×, 2×, 3× or 4×.

Counting in pieces

One recipe makes 50 brownies and you need 1,000 for an event? Enter the number of pieces and you immediately see what to weigh out.

Ready for mise en place

Print the converted recipe straight away, or open it on your phone in cook mode.
