Scale recipes to any yield

From a tasting for 8 to a dinner for 120, without a calculator and without mistakes.

  • One tapFrom ¼× to 10× with one button.
  • On what you haveConvert on the ingredient that is on the shelf.
  • The original stays“Original” puts everything back.

Convert in one go

Enter the yield you need and Savour converts every quantity. The original recipe stays as it is.

Understands units

Savour knows that 3 teaspoons make a tablespoon and 1000 grams a kilo. 20 teaspoons become 6 ⅔ tablespoons, and spoons and cups stay clean fractions instead of long decimals. Even without a yield, scale with one tap: ½×, 2×, 5× or 10×, or type your own factor, such as 25×. The yield at the top follows.

Counting in pieces

One recipe makes 50 brownies and you need 1,000 for an event? Enter the number of pieces and you immediately see what to weigh out.

Ready for mise en place

Print the converted recipe straight away, or open it on your phone in cook mode.

Scaling on what you have

Often you do not start from the number of guests but from what is on the shelf: 3 kilos of beetroot left, or one crate of shallots. Under "More scaling options", choose the ingredient and enter how much you have. The whole recipe follows, even if it is written in grams and you think in kilos.

What you still judge yourself

Maths is not the same as cooking. Taste salt, acidity and spices again in large batches. A bigger pan evaporates relatively less, so reduction times change. Savour gives you the right starting point; the cook finishes it. That is why the original recipe always stays.

Converting with one button

Under the title there are buttons from ¼× to 10×. All quantities follow, in tidy units: 3 decilitres of wine, 750 grams of butter.

More converting

Enter a factor, the yield you need, or what you have: “I have 3 kilos of shallots”. The whole recipe follows.

Frequently asked questions

Does the original recipe change?

No. Converting happens on screen; “Original” takes you back to the base recipe.

Can I convert by pieces or portions?

Yes. If the recipe has a yield, such as 50 pieces or 4 people, enter how many you need.

Can I print the converted recipe?

Yes. You print exactly the quantities you see on screen, or open them in cooking mode.
