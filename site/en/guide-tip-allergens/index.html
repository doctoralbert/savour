Allergens and fixed agreements

Know exactly what is in a dish when a guest asks.

In the ingredient list

In every recipe, list components with allergens clearly in the ingredients, also when they come from another base recipe.

Base recipes

For a composed dish, name the base recipes in it. Link them and one tap shows what is in the stock or sauce.

Notes

Put fixed agreements and allergen information in a note on the recipe, so they appear on the print too.
