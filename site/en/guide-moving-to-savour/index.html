Moving to Savour

Whichever app you come from: make an export, pick the file and your recipes are in Savour.

On this pageHow moving worksApps with their own importYour app is not listed?Cancelling your old appComparing

How moving works

Almost every recipe app can make an export: a file with all your recipes. Pick that file in Savour under “From another recipe app”. Savour recognises which app it came from by itself.

Apps with their own import

Each app has a page on how to make the export:

  • Paprika: .paprikarecipes and .paprikarecipe
  • Mela: .melarecipes and .melarecipe
  • Mealie: .zip with JSON
  • Tandoor: .json or .zip
  • Recipe Keeper: .zip
  • Copy Me That: .zip with HTML
  • Crouton: .zip with .crumb
  • MasterCook: .mx2 and .mxp
  • Cookmate: .mcb
  • Living Cookbook: .fdx and .fdxz
  • Evernote: .enex
  • Google Keep: .zip from Google Takeout
  • Flavorish: .zip
  • CookBookManager: .zip with .yml
  • Plan to Eat: .csv
  • Pepperplate: no export file

Your app is not listed?

Check whether your app can export as CSV, HTML, JSON (schema.org), Word, PDF or text; Savour almost always reads those. See Excel and CSV, JSON and schema.org and Text files. If it doesn’t work, get in touch and send a sample of your export.

Cancelling your old app

Keep your old app next to Savour for a while until you have checked everything. Keep the export file as a backup.
