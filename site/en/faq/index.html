Frequently asked questions

How do I add a recipe?

Go to Add recipe. You can enter a recipe yourself or import a source. After importing, you check the recognised details before saving.

Where are my recipes stored?

Your personal recipes belong to your own account. A recipe you remove from a folder is not automatically deleted from your personal library.

Can I share recipes?

Yes. With a shared folder you can share recipes with other users and, depending on the permissions, work together.

Can I change my folders?

Yes. You can create, rename and delete personal recipe folders. In Update recipe folders you decide which folders appear in the menu and in which order.

How does importing work?

You can use various source files and text. Imported recipes should always be checked before saving.

Can I decide my own structure?

Yes. Categories, personal folders and shared folders are meant to fit the way you work. Keep the library compact and use the detail page for the full recipe.

What should I check after an AI import?

Always check the title, quantities, units, yield and steps before using an imported recipe professionally.

What can I do for free?

With a free account you store up to 100 recipes, import Word, PDF and text with the Savour reader and can be a member of other people's shared folders. New accounts also try Savour Pro free for 7 days, without payment details; during that time you also store up to 100 recipes.

What do I get with Savour Pro?

Unlimited recipes, creating your own shared folders and inviting your team, and AI import of photos, handwritten and scanned recipes. For a whole kitchen there is a team plan: the bigger the team, the lower the price per person.

How do my team members get Pro?

You add them via My team in the menu, or on your phone via My account → Subscription. They get an invitation by email and have Savour Pro as soon as they log in or sign up with exactly that email address. You count as the first seat yourself.

What happens to my recipes if I stop Pro?

They all stay, just like your shared folders. You just cannot add new recipes above 100 or create new shared folders.
