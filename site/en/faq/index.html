Frequently asked questions

How do I add a recipe?

Go to Add recipe. You can enter a recipe yourself or import a source. After importing, you check the recognised details before saving.

Where are my recipes stored?

Your personal recipes belong to your own account. A recipe you remove from a folder is not automatically deleted from your personal library.

Can I share recipes?

Yes. With a kitchen team you can share recipes with other users and, depending on the permissions, work together.

Can I change my folders?

Yes. You can create, rename and delete personal recipe folders. In Update recipe folders you decide which folders appear in the menu and in which order.

How does importing work?

You can use various source files and text. Imported recipes should always be checked before saving.

Can I decide my own structure?

Yes. Categories, personal folders and kitchen teams are meant to fit the way you work. Keep the library compact and use the detail page for the full recipe.

What should I check after an AI import?

Always check the title, quantities, units, yield and steps before using an imported recipe professionally.

What can I do for free?

With a free account you store up to 100 recipes, import Word, PDF and text with the Savour reader and can be a member of other people's kitchen teams. New accounts also try Savour Pro free for 7 days, without payment details; during that time you also store up to 100 recipes.

What do I get with Savour Pro?

Unlimited recipes, creating your own kitchen teams and inviting your team, and AI import of photos, handwritten and scanned recipes. For a whole kitchen there is an team plan: the bigger the team, the lower the price per person.

How do the members of my organisation get Pro?

You add them via My team in the menu, or on your phone via My account → Subscription. They get an invitation by email and have Savour Pro as soon as they log in or sign up with exactly that email address. You count as the first seat yourself.

What happens to my recipes if I stop Pro?

They all stay, just like your kitchen teams. You just cannot add new recipes above 100 or create new kitchen teams. A kitchen team is kept, but can only be used again once the owner has Savour Pro.

Can you import my recipes for me?

Yes. With the import service, the chefs behind Savour personally move your whole recipe folder over: imported, organised in your folders and every recipe checked. From €85 one-off, together with a yearly Team 5, 10 or 25 plan. The first 5 kitchens are imported for free.

How does the import service work?

You request it on the website. Once approved you get a personal link: you pay the team plan and the package, then send us your recipes, your folder structure and the email addresses of your colleagues. We import and check everything. As soon as your team is ready, everyone gets an email to choose a password.

What happens to the files I send?

They are kept in private storage that only Savour can see, and are deleted together with your other details as soon as your team is ready. After that your recipes only live in your team in Savour.

Do my colleagues have to create an account?

No. With the import service we create the accounts for the email addresses you give us. Everyone gets Savour Pro through your team and an email to choose their own password.
