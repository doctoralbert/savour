Scaling

From 8 covers to 120 with one tap, without calculation errors.

On this pageQuick buttonsBack to the originalAnother factor or yieldThe original never changesPrinting scaledWithout an account tooTasting is still needed

Quick buttons

Every recipe with ingredients shows “Convert” with the buttons ¼×, ½×, 1½×, 2×, 3×, 4×, 5× and 10×. One tap and all quantities are converted, in every part of the recipe.

Back to the original

“Original” shows the original quantities again.

Another factor or yield

Need 2.5×, or exactly 3.5 litres? Or do you have 3 kg of one ingredient left? Choose ⋯ “More scaling options”. See More scaling options.

The original never changes

Scaling only changes what you see on screen. The saved recipe stays exactly as it was, for your team too.

Printing scaled

Print a scaled recipe and you print the scaled quantities. See Printing a recipe.

Without an account too

Anyone who opens a shared recipe can scale it, even without an account.

Tasting is still needed

Not everything scales one to one. What to watch out for is in Scaling without mistakes.
