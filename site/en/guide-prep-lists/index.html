Prep lists

What each station needs ready, ticked off per day, with your whole team.

SAVOUR

One list per station

A prep list is the list for your station: menus (à la carte, banqueting), a heading per course or dish and below it what needs to be ready. You read it from top to bottom, in the order of the menu.

List and cooking mode

List shows everything in Savour’s style, with a box next to each item. Ticking it marks it done for today, for your whole team. Cooking mode shows only the list, large, and keeps your screen on.

Covers and quantities

Fill in the number of covers next to the name of each menu; Savour works out how much you need of each item (40 covers × 60 g = 2.4 kg). Every day the list starts empty. With ⋯ Clear list you remove the ticks in between too.

Together in a kitchen team

Everyone in the team sees a prep list in a kitchen team. What a colleague ticks off appears straight away, with name and time. Anyone can tick off, even without Savour Pro. Without internet what you tap stays and is sent as soon as you’re connected again.

Importing

Already have your list in Excel, Word or on paper? Choose Import. Savour sorts it and you check everything before it is saved. A photo is read by the AI, with Savour Pro.

Print

Print the list on one A4, portrait or landscape, with boxes to tick, just like your Excel list. The date and covers are left off, so you can use it every day. With Savour Pro your restaurant’s logo is on it.

Savour Pro

Creating and managing prep lists works with Savour Pro. Anyone in the kitchen team can tick off, even with a free account. If you stop Pro, your lists are kept.
