What we are working on

Savour grows with the kitchen. This is on the roadmap. Missing something? Let us know.

  • FirstThe food cost calculator: cost per dish and per menu.
  • NextMenu engineering and the history of every recipe.
  • MaybeAn app of our own for iPhone and Android, if chefs want it.

Food cost calculator

See what each dish, each portion and your whole menu costs, with your supplier’s prices and your own discount. Read more.

Menu engineering

Which dishes sell well and earn well? Linked to your recipes and allergens.

History of every recipe

See who changed what in a shared recipe, and restore an earlier version when needed.

An app for iPhone and Android

Savour now works in the browser on every device. We are considering a separate app, and your feedback counts.

Why this order?

Recipes must be correct and easy to find first, which is why importing, folders and sharing came first. Cost and allergens build on that, because they are only reliable when the quantities in your recipes are right. A cost based on an incomplete recipe gives false certainty.

What you can prepare today

Write quantities in grams or millilitres and give every recipe a yield. Link base recipes to the dishes that use them, so you can see straight away which stock or sauce is inside. That already helps in the kitchen today, and it makes every next step easier.

Want to share ideas? Email us at contact@savour.page.

Latest launch: allergens

Since November 2026 every recipe shows its allergens, including through base recipes. See the feature or read how it works.

Frequently asked questions

When are these features coming?

There are deliberately no dates. A feature goes live when it is good enough for the rush of a service.

Will it cost extra?

Allergens are part of Savour Pro. We decide about the other features when they are ready.

Can I share ideas?

Please do. Email contact@savour.page and tell us which problem you want to solve in the kitchen.
