Cook mode

Only the recipe, large and readable, even with wet hands.

On this pageStarting cook modeScreen onTicking offCooking scaledOn the tablet at the pass

Starting cook mode

Choose “Cooking mode” at the top of a recipe. You see only the recipe, in large text, without the menu.

Screen on

In cook mode Savour tries to keep your screen on so you don’t have to keep touching it. Whether that works depends on your phone and browser.

Ticking off

Tick off ingredients you have weighed and steps that are done. After an interruption you see exactly where you were. You can tick off ingredients outside cook mode too.

Cooking scaled

Scale the recipe first with the quick buttons and then start cook mode: you cook with the scaled quantities.

On the tablet at the pass

Put Savour on a tablet as an app and use cook mode during mise en place. Recipes you have opened also work without internet.
