Features

My account: profile and password

Your profile and account settings in one place.

On this pageWhereProfilePasswordMore under AccountThe other tabs

Where

Choose Settings in the menu, or on your phone the My account icon at the top. My account has the tabs Account, Subscription and organisation, Appearance, Categories and Clean up.

Profile

Under Account you change your name and profile photo. You crop a profile photo so your face is nicely in view.

Password

Choose a new password under Account. If you no longer know your password, use “Forgot password?” when logging in.

More under Account

Here you print or download all your recipes, invite a colleague, install the app, log out and delete your account.

The other tabs

Subscription and organisation, Appearance, Categories and Clean up.
