Creating a kitchen team

One recipe book for your whole kitchen in five minutes.

On this pageCreating itSettingsAdding recipesInviting peopleWhere to start

Creating it

In the menu under Kitchen teams choose “Create kitchen team” and give it a name, for example your restaurant or kitchen. This needs Savour Pro, including the free trial week.

Settings

In the team settings you change the name and image under General. Under Share you find the invitation link, and under Members & permissions you manage who is in it.

Adding recipes

Choose “Add recipe” and tick recipes from your own library. The recipe stays yours: it is in the team and in your own recipes. See Recipes and folders in the team.

Inviting people

Send your team an invitation via WhatsApp, Telegram, email or the link. See Inviting your team.

Where to start

Start with the base recipes everyone uses daily, such as stocks, sauces and standard garnishes. Then expand by section or by menu.
