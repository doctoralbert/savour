Logging in and passwords

Logging in on your own phone, on a shared tablet at the pass or on a new computer.

On this pageLogging inStay signed inRemember passwordForgot passwordLogging outNot working?

Logging in

Choose “Log in” at the top right of savour.page and enter your email address and password. Your recipes, folders and kitchen teams are ready straight away, on every device you log in on.

Stay signed in

With “Stay signed in” ticked you stay logged in, even after closing the browser or app. It is also needed to open your recipes without internet. On a shared kitchen computer or tablet, leave it off: you are logged out as soon as the window closes.

Remember password

With “Remember password” your browser’s password manager may keep your password, so you don’t have to type it next time. Switch it off on a shared device.

Forgot password

Choose “Forgot password?” in the login window. You receive an email with a link. Click it, choose “Continue” in Savour and set a new password under My account. The link works once and for one hour.

Logging out

Log out under My account → Account. On your own phone there is no need; on a shared tablet there is, or leave “Stay signed in” off.
