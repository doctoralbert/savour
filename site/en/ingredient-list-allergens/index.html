Savour’s ingredient list

This is the list Savour reads to suggest allergens for a new product. Search an ingredient and see which allergens Savour suggests. The names are in Dutch, with English names where the source has them.

A suggestion, not a label

The allergens in this list are suggested by Savour, based on the sources and the name. A brand can contain something you do not expect: your supplier’s label always comes first. "No allergen known" does not mean an ingredient is allergen-free. In the app you confirm every product yourself, and Savour never says a recipe is free of allergens while there are ingredients without a checked product. Read how it works in Allergens in Savour.

Where the list comes from

Three sources together: the ingredient taxonomy of Open Food Facts (© Open Food Facts contributors, Open Database License 1.0), the Dutch product names from NEVO online version 2025/9.0, RIVM, Bilthoven (nevo-online.rivm.nl), the Wikipedia lists of vegetables, herbs, mushrooms, sauces, drinks and cheeses (names under CC BY-SA 4.0), Wikidata (CC0), and Savour’s own list of kitchen terms. NEVO contains no allergens: those are added by Savour, not data from RIVM. Spot a mistake? Let us know.
