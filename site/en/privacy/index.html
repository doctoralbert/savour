Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

1. What data

Using Savour may involve processing account data, profile data, recipes, folders, favourites and data about shared collaboration, among other things.

We occasionally send news about Savour to your account email address. You can unsubscribe with one click at the bottom of every email.

2. What data is used for

Data is used to make your account work, to store and display recipes, to provide folder and sharing features and to support the security and operation of Savour.

3. Recipes and kitchen teams

Your personal recipes are meant for your account. When you share a recipe or folder, other participants can see the content according to the permissions and settings of that shared feature.

In the Savour community, other members see the name from your profile next to your messages, never your email address. The administrator can hide or delete messages that do not belong there, such as spam or advertising. If you delete your account, your messages remain as “Deleted account”, without your name; if you want them gone completely, delete them yourself first or email us.

4. Automated processing

When you use import or AI features, the content you provide may be processed to turn it into structured recipe data. Check the results before use.

To recognise ingredients better, Savour looks at which ingredient names appear in recipes that it does not know yet. For this we only use the name, separate from the recipe, the quantity and your account. An administrator reviews these names; anything approved becomes part of Savour’s ingredient list and cannot be traced back to you or your recipe.

To improve the Savour reader, Savour anonymously counts how each import went: the type of source, whether the reader was unsure, whether AI was needed and how many lines you corrected afterwards. We store no recipe content and no account details with this.

If “Help improve the Savour reader” is on (My account → Account; on by default), Savour also shares the example itself when the reader is unsure or after you correct something: the text from your file, what the reader and the AI made of it and the recipe as you saved it. Text files only, never photos. Only the Savour team sees these examples; they are not shared with anyone else and are not used to train AI. If you turn it off, you can have your earlier examples removed straight away. If you delete your account, they are deleted too.

We also keep every file you import as an original in your own protected folder, so we can look back at a failed import and fix it. If “Help improve the Savour reader” is on, we also keep, for each imported recipe, the version as the reader or the AI read it and as you saved it (for a photo, only the text). This shows us what you correct afterwards. The Savour team reviews this daily, also with the help of AI tools, to check imports and improve the reader. It is not shared with anyone else and we do not use it to train AI. If you turn the setting off, you can have these versions removed; if you delete your account, they are deleted too.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account and available personal data via the account functions. Retention periods may depend on legal obligations and technical necessity.

6. Your rights

Depending on the applicable privacy law, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability.

7. Who is responsible

Savour is a Dutch company and is responsible for processing your data. Send questions about privacy or requests to access, correct or delete your data to contact@savour.page. Savour uses the following processors: Supabase (database and login), Vercel (website hosting), Stripe (payments), OpenAI (AI import, Savour Pro only) and Resend (email). Agreements have been made with these parties to protect your data; some also process data outside the European Union, on the basis of the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

8. Payments and AI

Payments for Savour Pro are processed by Stripe. Savour does not store payment details such as card numbers, only the status of your subscription. With AI import, the sources you provide are processed via OpenAI to turn them into recipe data.

9. Cookies and local storage

Savour uses functional storage in your browser to make the service work, for example to keep you logged in and remember your language and display (light or dark). We also count visits to the website anonymously, with a random code in your browser, so we can see how often Savour is used. No consent is required for this.

If you have an account, Savour keeps track of the days you use the app and whether that is on a phone, tablet or computer. This shows us how Savour is used in the kitchen and helps us spot misuse. We only store the type of device and the time, no browser details or IP address. If you delete your account, this data is deleted with it. We also store with your account how you found Savour, for example via Google or a shared link. We derive this from your browser’s anonymous visit code; the visits themselves are not linked to your account. New accounts are asked once, optionally, how they found Savour; we store that answer with your account too.

10. Changes

When Savour changes, we update this privacy policy. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. We will let you know about important changes.
