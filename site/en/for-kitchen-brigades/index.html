For kitchen brigades

From chef de partie to intern: your whole kitchen team cooks from the same recipe, in every restaurant you run.

  • The same recipe for everyoneFrom chef de partie to intern.
  • Permissions that make senseThe chef manages, the sous-chef edits, the cooks read.
  • Onboarded fasterNew people read at home how things are done here.

One version for everyone

Adjust a sauce for the new menu and everyone in the kitchen team works with the new version straight away.

Three roles, like in the kitchen

One owner runs the kitchen team, just like the chef. Sous-chefs add new dishes and edit recipes. Cooks read along on the line without accidentally deleting anything.

On your own phone

No more shared binder full of stains. Every member of the brigade opens the section’s recipes on their own phone, with large text in cook mode.

New people up to speed faster

Interns and new cooks read how things are done here, instead of having to ask during service. Being a member of a kitchen team is free; with a team plan everyone gets Savour Pro.

The first week with your team

Do not enter everything at once. Start with one section, or with the base recipes everyone needs daily. Let the team work with that for a week and collect what they miss. Only then add the rest. That makes it a tool of the brigade rather than an order from above.

Changing staff

Temporary staff, weekend help and cooks moving between sites often do not know where anything is. As viewers they can open the right recipes straight away, without anyone training them during service. When someone leaves, you remove them from the team and the recipes stay with the kitchen.

Everyone in their place

The Brigade screen shows who is in the team and with which role. When someone leaves, you remove them from the team; the recipes stay with the kitchen.

On their own phone

Every member opens the section’s recipes on their own phone, in large text in cooking mode. No more stained recipe folder.

Frequently asked questions

What does a kitchen team cost?

Being a member is free. You create the team itself with Savour Pro. With a team subscription for 5, 10 or 25 people (€8.99, €14.99 or €29.99 a month) everyone in the team gets Pro.

Does everyone need an account?

Yes, their own account, and it is free. Whoever is invited creates an account or logs in and is then a member.

Can someone only read along?

Yes. As a cook they view, print and convert, but change nothing in the recipes.
