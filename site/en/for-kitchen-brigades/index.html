For kitchen brigades

From chef de partie to intern: your whole kitchen team cooks from the same recipe, in every restaurant you run.

One version for everyone

Adjust a sauce for the new menu and everyone in the shared folder works with the new version straight away.

Rights per person

You manage the folder. Your sous-chef or chef de partie can add and edit recipes. Commis and interns can view but not change anything.

On your own phone

No more shared binder full of stains. Every member of the brigade opens the section’s recipes on their own phone, with large text in cook mode.

New people up to speed faster

Interns and new cooks read how things are done here, instead of having to ask during service. Being a member of a shared folder is free; with a team plan everyone gets Savour Pro.
