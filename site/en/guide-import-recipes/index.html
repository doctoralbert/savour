Importing recipes

Your whole recipe folder into Savour at once, whatever the source. Here you find how it works for each source.

On this pageWhere to startFiles and documentsPhotos and websitesMoving from another appAlways review firstFree and Savour ProNo time to do it yourself

Where to start

Choose “Add recipe” in the menu and then “Import recipe”. You see the sources you can import from. Each source opens a file picker that only shows matching files. You can also drag files onto the screen.

Files and documents

The Savour reader reads these sources, free and without AI:

  • Word: .doc and .docx, even with hundreds of recipes in one document.
  • PDF: digital PDFs. A scanned PDF is read by AI (Savour Pro).
  • Typing or pasting text: from an email, WhatsApp or a note.
  • Other text files: .txt, .md, .rtf, .odt, .html and Pages.
  • Spreadsheets and CSV: Excel or Google Sheets, saved as .csv.
  • JSON and schema.org: recipes in a structured format, and Savour files.
  • A whole folder or zip: everything mixed, in one go.

Photos and websites

Photos of handwritten or scanned recipes and the camera are read by Savour Pro’s AI: see Photos and notes. Bring in a recipe from a website with its link: see Recipe from a website.

Moving from another app

Savour reads exports from Paprika, Mela, Mealie, Tandoor, Recipe Keeper, Copy Me That, Crouton, MasterCook, Cookmate, Living Cookbook, Evernote, Google Keep, Flavorish, CookBookManager and Plan to Eat. Notes from Apple Notes, OneNote, Notion and other apps: see Notes apps. The overview is in Moving to Savour.

Always review first

Nothing is saved before you have seen it. The review screen shows per recipe what was read, and what Savour is unsure about. Savour never invents quantities, ingredients or steps.

Free and Savour Pro

A free account imports Word, PDF, text and exports from other apps, up to 100 recipes. If you have less room left than you import, Savour saves the first recipes that fit and the rest stay in the review screen. With Savour Pro you keep unlimited recipes and AI reads photos, scanned PDFs and recipes the reader has doubts about, up to 500 imports a month. See When the AI helps.

No time to do it yourself

With the import service, the chefs behind Savour move your whole recipe folder over for you.
