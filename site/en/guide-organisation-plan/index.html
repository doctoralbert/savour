Organisations: Pro for your whole kitchen

Savour Pro for everyone in your kitchen, with one subscription.

What it is

With an organisation everyone in your team has Savour Pro. There are plans for 5, 10 or 25 people: €8.99, €14.99 or €29.99 a month.

Setting it up

Choose an organisation on the Savour Pro page, or go to My account → Subscription and organisation. Invite your team to the seats there.

Organisation or kitchen team

An organisation is about who has Pro. A kitchen team is about which recipes you share. For a team that shares recipes, one owner with Pro is enough; with an organisation everyone can also have photos read, create kitchen teams and print with a logo.
