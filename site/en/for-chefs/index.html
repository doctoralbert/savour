For chefs

Whether you run the kitchen, make the sauces or work in pastry: your recipes have to be right and easy to find.

  • Precise to the gramYour recipe stays as you wrote it.
  • By covers and piecesFrom 8 to 120, from 50 bonbons to 800.
  • Your knowledge keptEven when you change kitchens.

Your recipes, one place

Seasonal menus, tasting menus, base recipes from years ago. In Savour it is all in one recipe book, arranged the way you work.

Precise, to the gram

In pastry a recipe is a formula. Savour keeps your recipes exactly as you wrote them and never invents quantities. If the reader is unsure during an import, you check it yourself.

Scale by covers and pieces

From a tasting for 8 to a dinner for 120, or from 50 bonbons to 800. Savour converts everything.

Your knowledge kept

What you have built up over the years no longer lives only in a notebook or in your head. It stays, even when you change kitchens.

For the chef writing the menu

At a menu change you want to look back at what was on last year and what sold well. Keep old menus in an archive folder and search by ingredient, and your autumn dishes are back in no time. Add a note about what you would do differently next time.

For the pastry chef

Pastry is about exact ratios and large batches. Scale on the ingredient you have, such as the chocolate left on the shelf, and split a recipe into parts like dough, filling and glaze. Steps count per part, so it stays readable on the bench.

Cooking from the phone

In cooking mode only the recipe is on screen, in large text, and the screen stays on. Tick off what is done.

Converting without a calculator

Pick 3× or enter the yield you need. All quantities follow; the original recipe stays as it was.

Frequently asked questions

What does Savour cost?

Free up to 100 recipes. Savour Pro costs €2.99 a month or €29 a year, for unlimited recipes, kitchen teams, AI import and printing with your logo.

Is Savour for home cooks too?

It can be, but Savour is made for the professional kitchen: large recipe books, teams and service.

Do my recipes stay if I stop?

Yes. Your recipes are always yours; if you stop Pro, everything stays.
