Digitise your recipes without retyping

Your recipe notebooks, Word files and coffee-stained notes become one organised recipe book in one go.

Word and PDF in one go

Drop a Word file or PDF into Savour, even if it holds hundreds of recipes. The Savour reader works out where each recipe starts and ends, and puts the title, ingredients and method in the right place. Numbers before titles and small typos such as 18oc instead of 180°C are cleaned up. One chef imported 1,400 recipes this way in about two minutes.

Handwritten recipes and photos

Take a photo of a note, a page from your notebook or a scan from an old binder. The AI reads the handwriting and turns it into a clean recipe, even when the photo is crooked or the paper is stained. This is part of Savour Pro.

Nothing made up

A recipe has to be right. Savour never invents quantities, ingredients or steps. If the reader is unsure, the recipe is flagged and you check it yourself before it is saved.

Then simply carry on

Every imported recipe can be edited, given a photo, put in a folder and shared with your brigade.
