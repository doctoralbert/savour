Digitise your recipes without retyping

Your recipe notebooks, Word files and coffee-stained notes become one organised recipe book in one go.

  • Whole folders at onceWord and PDF with hundreds of recipes become separate recipes.
  • Handwritten tooWith Savour Pro the AI reads photos of notes and notebooks.
  • Nothing inventedDoubt is marked; you decide before anything is saved.

Word and PDF in one go

Drop a Word file or PDF into Savour, even if it holds hundreds of recipes. The Savour reader works out where each recipe starts and ends, and puts the title, ingredients and method in the right place. Numbers before titles and small typos such as 18oc instead of 180°C are cleaned up. One chef imported 1,400 recipes this way in about two minutes.

Handwritten recipes and photos

Take a photo of a note, a page from your notebook or a scan from an old binder. The AI reads the handwriting and turns it into a clean recipe, even when the photo is crooked or the paper is stained. This is part of Savour Pro.

Order in large, messy recipe folders

A binder with hundreds of recipes from different chefs, with numbers, loose headings and recipes that start halfway down a page? Savour splits them up itself, recognises parts such as dough and filling, corrects common spelling mistakes and suggests a category for each recipe. Recipes the reader is unsure about are read again by the AI with Savour Pro. You check everything in one overview before it is saved.

Nothing made up

A recipe has to be right. Savour never invents quantities, ingredients or steps. If the reader is unsure, the recipe is flagged and you check it yourself before it is saved.

From a website or another app

Paste the link to a recipe on a website and Savour pulls out the recipe. Moving from another recipe app, such as Paprika, Mealie, Tandoor, Recipe Keeper, Crouton, MasterCook, Copy Me That or Plan to Eat? Make an export there and bring the file into Savour in one go.

Then simply carry on

Every imported recipe can be edited, given a photo, put in a folder and shared with your brigade.

One button for everything

Under “Add recipe” you choose import or enter it yourself. Import works with Word, PDF, text, a photo or an export from another app, even with hundreds of recipes at once.

A recipe book straight away

After saving, your recipes are in one place, with A to Z and search. Then put them in folders and share them with your brigade.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can Savour read?

Word, PDF and text, a link to a recipe on a website, and exports from other apps such as Paprika, Mealie and Recipe Keeper. With Savour Pro also photos and handwritten notes.

Does importing cost anything?

You read Word, PDF and text for free with the Savour reader, up to 100 recipes. With Savour Pro you keep unlimited recipes and the AI reads photos and doubtful cases, up to 500 a month.

What happens when the reader is unsure?

Then you see that recipe marked and check it yourself before it is saved. Savour never fills in anything that is not there.
