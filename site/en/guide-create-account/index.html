Creating an account

Free, no payment details, and done within a minute.

Create your account

Choose “Start for free” on savour.page. Enter your first name, last name, email address and a password and choose “Create account”. No payment details needed.

Confirm your email address

You receive an email with a link to confirm your account. Click the link and then “Continue” in Savour. No email? Check your spam folder. If you try to log in without confirming, Savour offers to send a new confirmation email. A link from an email works once and for one hour; if you request several, only the newest works.

Seven days of Savour Pro

Every new account gets 7 days of Savour Pro for free. You can have photos of notes read, create a kitchen team and print with your logo. During the trial you keep up to 100 recipes. After the trial week you keep everything you made.

Invited by a colleague

If a colleague sent you a link, Savour opens “Create account” straight away. You get the same trial week, and your colleague gets 14 days of Pro as a thank-you once you have confirmed your email address and logged in. If you received a kitchen team invitation, you join that team after logging in. See Inviting a colleague and Inviting your team.

Email address already has an account

If Savour says the email address already has an account, no new confirmation email is sent. Log in instead, or choose “Forgot password?” if you no longer know the password.

Next

After confirming you see the welcome screen with a short checklist. Start importing or enter a recipe yourself. A plan for the first days is in Your first week with Savour.
