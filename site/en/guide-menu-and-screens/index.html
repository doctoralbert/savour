The menu and screens

What is in the menu and where to find what, on a computer and on your phone.

On this pageRecipesRecipe foldersKitchen teamsGeneralAt the topHelp on every screenOn your phone

Recipes

The top of the menu holds your own recipes:

  • All recipes: your whole library, numbered and in alphabetical order.
  • Favourites: recipes with a star.
  • Categories: recipes by kind, such as sauces or pastry.
  • Add recipe: import or enter one yourself.

Recipe folders

Below that are your own folders, for example by section or by menu, with “Add recipe folder” to make one. You choose which folders appear in the menu and in what order.

Kitchen teams

Here are the kitchen teams you belong to, with “Create kitchen team” to start your own (Savour Pro).

General

At the bottom are Savour Pro, Invite a colleague, Install app and Settings, where you find your account, subscription, appearance, categories and clean-up.

At the top

In the bar at the top you search your recipes, switch between the dark and light theme with one button and choose the language: English, Dutch, German, Spanish or French.

Help on every screen

Many screens have an i button next to the title. It opens a short explanation of what you can do on that screen, inside the app.

On your phone

On a phone a bar at the bottom holds Recipes, Recipe folders, Kitchen teams and Add recipe. At the top you search, open your favourites and go to My account. Recipes open large and readable; cook mode shows only the recipe.
