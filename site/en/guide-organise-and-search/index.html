Organising and searching

Find your beurre blanc in three seconds, even among hundreds of recipes.

On this pageAll recipesFour ways to organiseCleaning upWith your teamExamples

All recipes

“All recipes” holds all your own recipes, numbered and in alphabetical order. The letters A–Z jump to a first letter, and the categories at the top show just one kind of recipe.

Four ways to organise

Savour has four ways, used side by side:

  • Search: by name, ingredient, component or technique.
  • Categories: what a recipe is, such as sauces or pastry. Each recipe has one.
  • Folders: how you work, such as by section, menu or season. A recipe can be in several folders.
  • Favourites: the recipes you use most.

Cleaning up

After a big import, Clean up removes numbers in front of recipe names and duplicate recipes.

With your team

Only you see your own folders. To share recipes with your brigade, use a kitchen team with its own folders.

Examples

Ideas for a structure are in Recipe folder examples and in the tip Categories and folders.
