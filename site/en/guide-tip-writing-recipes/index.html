A recipe everyone makes the same way

A good kitchen recipe leaves no room for interpretation.

On this pageName and yieldIngredientsMethodCritical pointsRead more

Name and yield

Start with a clear name and the yield: how many litres, how many pieces or for how many covers.

Ingredients

List all ingredients in the order you use them, with quantities in grams or millilitres rather than spoons or cups. See Ingredients and quantities.

Method

Write the method in short steps, one action per step. See Writing the method.

Critical points

Always note the critical points: temperatures, times, the desired thickness or colour and what to watch out for. “Reduce by two thirds” or “core temperature 58 °C” prevents more mistakes than a nice description.
