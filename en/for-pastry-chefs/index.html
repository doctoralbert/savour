For pastry chefs

In pastry a recipe is a formula. One wrong gram and it fails.

Recipes that are right

Savour keeps your recipes exactly as you wrote them and never invents quantities. If the reader is unsure during an import, you check it yourself.

Scale by pieces

From one tray to twenty, or from 50 bonbons to 800. Savour converts everything.

The binder always up to date

Print your whole folder of ice creams, doughs or glazes at once, so the latest version is always on the wall.
