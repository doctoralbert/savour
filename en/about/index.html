The story of Savour

Savour was not dreamt up by a software company that spotted an opportunity in hospitality. It was made by chefs who kept losing their own recipes.

Here is the story as it happened: from the first notebook to the app you use today.

01The first notebook

It starts like it does for almost every cook: as an apprentice you write everything down. Sauces, garnishes, what you taste and what you would do differently. One notebook becomes two, then five. Those notebooks still exist, yellowed and full of stains. Some pages can no longer be read, not even by the person who wrote them.

02Years in the kitchen

Fifteen years of cooking, from commis to chef. The recipes move along: to loose sheets, to photos on three different phones, to messages in the group chat. Everything exists somewhere, but never at the moment you need it.

03The printer

As a chef you eventually put everything in Word. Hundreds of recipes in separate files. When the pastry section needs a new binder, someone from front of house prints them one by one, fifty times. That is where the idea starts: there has to be a better way.

04Savour

Savour is built after service and at weekends. Tested on our own recipes, in our own kitchen: 1,400 recipes from Word in two minutes, coffee-stained notes that become clean recipes, and folders the whole brigade can open.

05What comes next

Cost per recipe, allergens, recipe cards with your own logo. We build what chefs actually need, so we would love to hear what you are missing.
