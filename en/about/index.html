About Savour

Savour is a professional environment for managing, organising and sharing recipes.

What is Savour?

Savour has been developed for professional kitchens and kitchen teams. The goal is simple: store, find, organise and share recipes clearly, without unnecessary information.

Why Savour exists

In almost every kitchen, recipes are scattered: in a binder in the office, on loose notes at the pass, in Word files from a previous chef and in messages on phones. Anyone looking for a recipe often finds three versions with different quantities. Savour is made by chefs who run into this every day, with one goal: one place where every recipe is exactly as intended.

Manage recipes

You can enter recipes yourself or import existing ones. Your own recipe library stays central, and recipes can be organised in personal and shared folders.

From scribble to recipe

You do not have to retype existing recipes. The Savour reader extracts recipes from Word, PDF and text files, even when there are hundreds in one document. With Savour Pro, the AI also reads photos of handwritten notes and scanned pages. Savour never invents quantities or steps: anything uncertain is highlighted so you can check it before it is saved.

On every device

In the office you organise your recipes on the computer; in the kitchen you use the same library on your phone or tablet. Scaling recipes to a different yield, a cooking view with large text and printing recipe cards are all included.

For professionals

Savour is designed from the daily practice of chefs and professional kitchens. The interface focuses on recipes, organisation and collaboration.

By chefs, for chefs

Savour is made by chefs from the Netherlands. We know from experience where things go wrong in the kitchen: lost recipes, different versions and hours of retyping. Savour was built because we wanted a place that solves this ourselves, and one we use every day. Questions, ideas and feedback are always welcome via the contact page.

A central place for the kitchen

From loose notes and old documents to verified recipes: Savour brings information together in a clear workspace. You decide how to arrange categories and folders, so the structure fits your kitchen.

Working with a team

Shared folders let you make recipes available to colleagues in a controlled way. Use your personal library for your own work and shared folders for collaboration, handovers and agreed ways of working.
